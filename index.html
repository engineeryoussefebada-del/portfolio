Suspension System

The suspension system connects the vehicle’s wheels to the chassis. It absorbs shocks and vibrations from the road, improving ride comfort, stability, and handling. It usually includes components such as springs, shock absorbers, and control arms.

V12 Engine

A V12 engine is an internal combustion engine that has twelve cylinders arranged in two banks forming a V shape. It is known for producing high power and very smooth performance, and it is commonly used in luxury cars and high-performance vehicles.

Jet Engine

A jet engine is a type of propulsion system used in aircraft. It works by drawing in air, compressing it, mixing it with fuel, and igniting the mixture. The high-speed exhaust gases exit the engine and produce thrust that pushes the aircraft forward.

Car Jack

A car jack is a mechanical or hydraulic device used to lift a vehicle off the ground. It is commonly used during maintenance tasks such as changing a tire or repairing parts under the vehicle.
